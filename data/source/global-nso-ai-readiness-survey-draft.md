# Global NSO AI-Readiness Survey

## Instructions

Please answer for the national statistical office (NSO) only. The survey is
intended for one respondent with a general understanding of the NSO's work.
You may consult another colleague if needed, but a formal assessment meeting
is not required.

For Questions 8-11, first name one suitable public data product from the NSO.
This may be an indicator database, census or survey product, microdata product,
geospatial product, or another major public data product. A link is optional.
Use the same product for all four questions.

`Unknown` means that the respondent cannot determine the answer. `Not
applicable` is available only for questions whose subject genuinely depends on
current or planned AI activity. Neither response is a readiness level.

## Gateway

### G1. Organizational AI activity

Which best describes the NSO's current or planned use of AI for statistical
production or dissemination?

- No current or planned activity
- Planning or early exploration
- Active development or piloting
- Production use

G1 is not scored. It is used to interpret and route the scored questions.

### G2. Staff use of AI tools

Which best describes staff use of AI tools in the NSO, whether tools are
officially provided or not? Consider both use for statistical work and general
productivity. Answer both parts.

**Part A: Use pattern**

- No known staff use
- Personal or otherwise unapproved tools are used, but no enterprise-provided
  tools are used
- Enterprise-provided tools are used, but personal or otherwise unapproved use
  is not known
- Both enterprise-provided and personal or otherwise unapproved tools are used

**Part B: Governance of use**

- No known guidance or governance
- Informal or limited guidance exists
- Formal guidance has been communicated to relevant staff
- Use is managed and monitored under formal controls
- Unknown

G2 is not scored. Report both parts as an adoption and governance profile
alongside the readiness profile.

## Scored Questions

Unless otherwise stated, select one of: **Not in place**, **Early or partial**,
**Established**, or **Advanced or systematic**.

### Q1. Formal AI strategy

To what extent has the NSO adopted a formal AI strategy for statistical work?

- Not in place: No formal AI strategy exists.
- Early or partial: AI strategy discussions are underway or a draft exists.
- Established: A formal AI strategy has been adopted.
- Advanced or systematic: A formal strategy is adopted, implemented, and
  monitored.

### Q2. AI-specific legal and privacy guidance

To what extent does the NSO have formal AI-specific legal and privacy guidance
that relevant staff know about and can follow?

- Not in place: No AI-specific legal or privacy guidance exists.
- Early or partial: Guidance is informal, partial, or available only for some
  activities.
- Established: Formal guidance exists, but staff awareness or application is
  uneven.
- Advanced or systematic: Formal guidance is known, followed, and reviewed.

### Q3. AI-literacy training and guidance

What AI-literacy training or guidance has the NSO provided to staff?

- Not in place: No organizational AI-literacy guidance or training has been
  provided.
- Early or partial: Informal or occasional awareness activities have been
  provided.
- Established: Structured training has been provided to selected staff or
  relevant groups.
- Advanced or systematic: Structured training is provided systematically across
  relevant staff and is maintained over time.

### Q4. Specialized in-house expertise

To what extent does the NSO have specialized AI expertise in-house to develop,
assess, and maintain AI systems?

- Not in place: No specialized AI expertise is available in-house.
- Early or partial: A few individuals have relevant skills, but coverage is
  limited and concentrated.
- Established: In-house expertise covers some important technical areas or
  projects, with material gaps remaining.
- Advanced or systematic: An in-house team covers the main AI lifecycle needs,
  including development, assessment, deployment, and maintenance.

### Q5. Current technical capacity

What is the NSO's current technical capacity to support AI-related work?

- Not in place: Basic infrastructure is unreliable, including frequent
  electricity or internet disruption.
- Early or partial: Infrastructure is reliable enough for experimentation but
  is limited for operational use.
- Established: Secure infrastructure supports operational AI development, with
  some deployment or monitoring capability.
- Advanced or systematic: Secure end-to-end capacity supports deployment,
  monitoring, maintenance, and controlled scaling.

### Q6. Funding and resources

What best describes funding for the NSO's current or planned AI activities?

- Not in place: No funding is available.
- Early or partial: Some ad-hoc funding is available.
- Established: Funding is available for an approved AI pilot.
- Advanced or systematic: Sustained funding covers the full lifecycle,
  including operation, maintenance, and monitoring.
- Not applicable: The NSO has no current or planned AI activity.

### Q7. AI-specific partnerships

To what extent does the NSO have AI-specific partnerships that provide access
to expertise, methods, technology, implementation support, or responsible-AI
advice? Do not count ordinary statistical partnerships.

- Not in place: No relevant AI-specific partnership exists.
- Early or partial: Informal or occasional AI-specific support is available.
- Established: Active partnerships provide recurring access to AI capability.
- Advanced or systematic: Governed partnerships build sustained institutional
  capability and transfer knowledge to the NSO.

### Q8. Metadata standards and classifications

For the selected product, how is metadata documented and structured?

- Not in place: Little or no metadata is available.
- Early or partial: Metadata is mainly human-readable or produced ad hoc.
- Established: Recognized metadata standards are used for some or key parts of
  the product.
- Advanced or systematic: Maintained, machine-readable, standardized metadata
  covers the product.
- Unknown

### Q9. Quality control and provenance

For the selected product, how are quality, provenance, revisions, and known
limitations documented and communicated?

- Not in place: These matters are not documented systematically.
- Early or partial: Some narrative or partial documentation is available.
- Established: Systematic documentation is maintained for the product.
- Advanced or systematic: Structured, machine-readable quality and provenance
  information is linked to the product.
- Unknown

### Q10. Searchable data catalogue

How can users discover the selected product?

- Not in place: There is no central inventory or reliable discovery route.
- Early or partial: Users rely on static webpages, publication lists, or basic
  search.
- Established: A maintained catalogue supports structured search or filtering.
- Advanced or systematic: A comprehensive, machine-accessible catalogue is the
  authoritative discovery route.
- Unknown

### Q11. API access

How can users access the selected product programmatically?

- Not in place: No API is available.
- Early or partial: A pilot or limited API exists.
- Established: An API covers the selected product.
- Advanced or systematic: A documented and reliable API provides the product
  and relevant metadata.
- Unknown

### Q12. Agentic AI access and GenAI dissemination

To what extent is the NSO using or preparing for AI-mediated dissemination or
access by AI agents, with appropriate safeguards?

- Not in place: A current or planned relevant activity exists, but no
  AI-mediated access or dissemination capability or safeguards are in place.
- Early or partial: Awareness, discussion, or early exploration is underway.
- Established: An AI-mediated dissemination or agent-access pilot is underway.
- Advanced or systematic: Governed production use includes monitoring and
  human oversight.
- Not applicable: There is no current or planned activity relevant to this
  question.
- Unknown

## Optional Evidence

Where convenient, respondents may provide a public URL or document reference
for a strategy, guidance, training program, product catalogue, API, or license.
Evidence links are optional and do not affect the response score directly.
